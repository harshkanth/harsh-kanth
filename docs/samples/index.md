## What to look for

When you read these samples, notice the patterns I apply across all my documentation:

- **One task per page.** Each guide has a single goal stated in its first sentence.
- **Prerequisites up front.** Readers know what they need before they start, not halfway through.
- **Numbered steps with one action each.** UI labels are in bold and match the product exactly.
- **A result after each procedure.** Readers can confirm that the task worked.
- **Troubleshooting close to the task.** The most common failures are covered on the same page.
