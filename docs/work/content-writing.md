## Why it matters for technical work

Documentation tells a reader what to do. Content writing has to make them want to keep reading first. Writing for brands taught me to find the angle in a technical subject, to match a voice that isn't my own, and to respect a reader who can leave at any line. Those habits carry straight into product briefs, store listings, release announcements, and the introductions that decide whether anyone reads the rest of a guide.

## How I approach it

- **Find the reader's question first.** A good article answers something the reader already wonders about, then earns the right to go further.
- **Learn the voice before writing.** For ghostwriting, I study how the person or brand already speaks and write inside those limits.
- **Research like documentation.** Claims are checked, numbers are sourced, and technical details are confirmed with subject matter experts.
- **Write for search without writing for robots.** Keywords shape structure and headings; they don't replace clear sentences.
